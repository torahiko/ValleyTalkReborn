#GENEROUS GUS:
A Content Patcher mod that makes Gus a little more generous to new players.

##:: REQUIREMENTS ::
+ SMAPI
+ Content Patcher

##:: FEATURES ::

This mod will make Gus:
* Offer food to new players (1x per day, for the first two weeks)
* Give food to players who are at 200 gold or less (1x per day)
* Serve warm soup to the player after their first mine death
* Celebrate upcoming births/adoptions with the player (1x per day during expecting period)
* Congratulate new couples with a wedding gift!
